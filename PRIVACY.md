# Privacy Policy for clAIre

Last updated: September 25, 2026

## 1. Overview
clAIre is an open-source application designed with privacy as a core principle. The app runs locally on your machine and does not route telemetry, screen captures, or prompts through any project-owned intermediate servers.

## 2. Data Handled by the Application
* **Screen Captures & Prompts:** User-initiated screen grabs and input prompts are transmitted directly from your device to your configured API provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama instances).
* **API Credentials & Settings:** Provider keys, global shortcuts, and configuration settings are stored strictly on your local device within native OS configuration paths.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Requests sent to third-party endpoints are governed by their respective privacy policies:
* [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
* [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)

## 4. Local Storage & Retention
All active session history and cached screenshot files remain on your local filesystem and can be cleared at any time by resetting your session or clearing application data.

## 5. Contact
For privacy questions or concerns, please open a GitHub issue or contact voltrexium@gmail.com.
